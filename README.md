# FAMA Blueprint for Success
*From Prospect to Satisfied Client*

This repository documents the **FAMA Blueprint for Success**, a structured framework guiding prospects into becoming satisfied clients. It is organized into phases, checklists, and actionable steps.

---

## 📌 Process Timeline

The journey is divided into five sequential stages:

1. **Client Getting**  
2. **Onboarding**  
3. **Pre-Launch**  
4. **Launch**  
5. **Post-Launch**

## 📞 Discovery Call Preparation

- Reliable Internet Connection

- Dress well

- Good lighting

- Quiet environment

- Relaxed mind - don't be nervous!

*Remember the client needs you to help him / her solve his / her problem. You're here so you can know exactly how you can help - because every business is different.*

*Discovery call is where you uncover the problems so you can craft a sound strategy.*

---

## 📊 Strategy Audit / Proposal

After the call:

If the prospect has been running ads before

- Say you need to do an audit of the ad account so you know exactly where the problem is

- Ask for Ad Account View Only access

- Provide your email where he / she can send the invitation

- Do not let the call end without you receiving the invitation

- If the prospect is not in front of a computer, then it's okay but make sure to ask when he / she can do it and send a follow up on that exact date and time that he/ she mentioned.

## 🚀 Pre-Launch Phase 2

1️⃣ Prepare Assets

  Decide on which avatar/s to target first and decide. At least 2-3 ads for:
      - Unaware
      - Problem-aware
      - Solution-aware
      - Product-aware
      - Most aware

  [PROMPTS HERE](https://github.com/aarmintia/FAMAElite/blob/main/FAMA%20VIP%20AI%20Prompt_%20Create%20Meta%20Ad%20Creatives%20Based%20on%20the%205%20Levels%20of%20Awareness.docx)
    
2️⃣ Know Your KPIs

  *For ecom*
  - [BER Calculator](https://github.com/aarmintia/FAMAElite/blob/main/Copy%20of%20ROAS%20CPA%20Calculator%20%5BContent%20Upgrade%5D.xlsx)

  - Sometimes KPI is CPA or CAC

  *For Lead Gen use this formula*
  
  🔴 Target Revenue = Target Leads x CVR x Value of Each Conversion

  🔵 Target Leads = Target Revenue / CVR x Value of Each Conversion

  🟢 Target CPL = Allowable Budget / Target Leads
  
  3️⃣ Know Your Benchmarks
  
  *For old accounts*
  - Always aim for something better than the 3-6 months averages on:
    - ROAS / CPA / CPL
    - CPC / CPM / CTR
  
  *For new accounts*
  - Look for industry benchmarks

  4️⃣ Ideal Budget
  - How much are you willing to spend and how much time can you spend without earning profit?
  - How much are you currently earning in profit? How much of it can you invest in ads?
  - AOV x 5 (no ads to start with) x 50 (events to get out of learning)

## 🎯 Launch

1️⃣ Create Campaign on Ads Manager

  *For old accounts*
  - Start with historical best performers:
  - 2 best audiences + 1 broad + 3 best ads if best audience is broad, then just use broad

  *For new accounts*
  - Start with CBO-broad, 1 ad set, 3-6 ads
    
2️⃣ Test & Learn

  Run campaign for 5–7 days  

3️⃣ Analyze

  #### Hard metrics
  - SPEND, ROAS / CPA / AOV for Ecom
  - Leads, CPL or Messages, Cost per Message for Lead Gen

  And based on the hard metrics, diagnose what went wrong / right based on soft metrics:
  - CPM, CPC, CTR, as well as
  - Hook rate, Ave watch for videos
 
    [PROMPTS HERE](https://github.com/aarmintia/FAMAElite/blob/main/FAMA%20VIP%20AI%20Prompt_%20Analyze%20My%20Meta%20Ads%20Using%20the%20F.A.S.T.%20Metrics%20Framework.docx)
    
4️⃣ Optimize

  Turn off ads that had negative impact on business and iterate on winners
